SustainOpt: An Intelligent Corporate Sustainability Decision Support Platform
Project Vision

SustainOpt is an AI-powered Decision Support Platform that assists organizations in selecting the most sustainable business strategy by analyzing environmental, economic, social, and operational factors.

The platform enables decision-makers to compare multiple alternatives, evaluate sustainability metrics, predict long-term impacts, and receive intelligent recommendations using a novel decision optimization framework.

Problem Statement

Organizations struggle to make sustainability decisions because they must balance environmental impact, financial cost, operational efficiency, regulatory compliance, and social responsibility.

Existing solutions often analyze only one or two aspects and lack intelligent decision support.

SustainOpt addresses this challenge by integrating multiple sustainability dimensions into a unified AI-assisted decision-making platform.

Objectives
Build an intelligent sustainability decision support platform.
Compare multiple decision alternatives.
Calculate sustainability scores.
Predict long-term impacts.
Recommend the best alternative.
Visualize sustainability metrics through interactive dashboards.
Generate professional sustainability reports.
